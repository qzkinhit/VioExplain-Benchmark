# Limitations and unresolved claims

1. The public-data AEC adapter builds signatures from dimension sets. With every matchable violation required to be covered, its explained dimensions coincide with those matchable violation dimensions. This adapter cannot by itself demonstrate improved channel localization over violation-only evidence. Selected event representations and explanation cost remain distinct outputs.
2. The historical `publicexp/constraints.py` correlation constraint fits a slope without a separate intercept, while comparing raw residuals against a centered residual standard deviation. Nonzero offsets can cause systematic false violations. This module is retained for historical compatibility. Formal work must use a separately identified corrected extractor or disable correlation constraints; silently changing the historical scores is not acceptable.
3. The original APIs include prototype assumptions and synthetic study generators. The validated public entry point is `vioexplain.api.explain`. Its successful status establishes a feasible covering explanation under the supplied knowledge, not a verified physical cause.
4. Unknown evidence remains observable. Neither a zero matchable-subproblem cost nor a full-cover result establishes event identifiability. Approximation quality, stability of cost, uniqueness of selected events, and physical diagnosis are different claims.
5. The prior foundation-model studies include negative results. A combined frozen matching head improved one in-domain comparator but did not yield a cross-domain improvement, and normal false explanations remained substantial. The joint latent prototype also lost to inexpensive controls.
6. The oracle temporal-repair study supplies the true affected support and clean past values. Its predictive benefits are conditional upper-bound evidence, require future observations for delayed verification, and do not establish a deployable diagnostic system.
7. Formal tests of native event diagnosis, foundation-model necessity, out-of-domain generalization, incomplete knowledge, and independent multi-seed performance remain protocol-specific. A public repository is not proof that those claims have been completed.

The completed locked TEP event panel adds a direct event-level comparison. Frozen Chronos-2 improves its matched MinExplain raw-temporal control but loses to strong statistical classifiers; normal false explanations remain high. This panel uses given fault intervals and empirical labeled signatures, and does not validate end-to-end diagnosis or knowledge-update correctness.
